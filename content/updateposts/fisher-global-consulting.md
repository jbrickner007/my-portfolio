For the month of May, I was the project manager and technical lead for a team of 3 students consulting with the international marketing agency for the region. After assessing the company, we made a recommendation to our client to implement an AI scoring system for leads, and I developed the initial model and workflow for them. 

We presented our findings and recommendations to the CEO and Frankfurt office of the company.
